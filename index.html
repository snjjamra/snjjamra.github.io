About Us

We Create Architectural Excellence

Premier Architectural Firm in Ghaziabad NCR

Art Designs Architects & Designers is a premier architectural firm based in Kavi Nagar Industrial Area, Ghaziabad NCR, founded in 2019 by two visionary architects from NIT Bhopal - Tarun Bansal and Sanjay Jamra.

With a passion for innovative design, our team brings together creativity and technical expertise to deliver projects that exceed expectations across residential, commercial, industrial, office, interior, and landscape design.
