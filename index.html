About Us

We Create Architectural Excellence

Premier Architectural Firm with 8+ Years of Experience

Art Designs Architects & Designers is a premier architectural firm based in Ghaziabad NCR, founded by two visionary architects from NIT Bhopal - Tarun Bansal and Sanjay Jamra.

With a passion for innovative design, our team brings together creativity and technical expertise to deliver projects that exceed expectations across residential, commercial, and interior design.
